The generated gazetteer contains information from:

- **GeoNames** — https://www.geonames.org/ — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Derived from the cities1000 dump, normalized and deduplicated.
- **© OpenStreetMap contributors** — https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright — Open Database License 1.0, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . OSM inputs in the reference build are provided through **OSMNames v2.0.4**, https://osmnames.org/ . Filtered and deduplicated; geographic room anchors were added.
- **Natural Earth** — https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ — public-domain generalized countries, administrative areas, geographic regions and marine polygons, v5.1.2.
- **DataSF, City and County of San Francisco** — [Analysis Neighborhoods](https://data.sf.gov/d/p5b7-5n3h) — neighborhood boundaries, Open Data Commons Public Domain Dedication and License.
- **U.S. Census Bureau** — [TIGERweb Incorporated Places](https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/tigerWMS_ACS2026/MapServer/28) — 2026 California municipal boundaries.
